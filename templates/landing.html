Type, speak in any language, or upload a prescription or skin photo. Care Companion checks every answer against its ChromaDB knowledge base before using Gemini — so it never hallucinates.

📚

RAG over PDF Directory (NEW)

Directory: knowledge_base/ reads whole directory + subdirectories. Add PDFs, TXT, MD anywhere. Auto-chunked 1000 chars / 150 overlap with source + page metadata. Retrieved via ChromaDB, citations like (Source: fever_management.pdf p.1). LLM generates grounded answer using RAG.

💬

Text + RAG Verification

Every answer first searches RAG directory (ChromaDB). Retrieves top 5 relevant chunks with source citations. If relevant, Gemini uses ONLY those chunks. If not, fallback to general safe info. Prevents hallucination.

🎤

Voice in your language

Faster Whisper Large-v3 transcribes your voice. Responds in the language you picked in profile — Hindi, Marathi, Spanish, Tamil, Telugu & 6 more. RAG still used for voice queries.

📸

Prescription Parser

Upload image or PDF. Extracts Medicine name, Dosage, Frequency, Duration, Route, Instructions + auto precaution. Then one-click add to scheduler with email reminders.

🩺

Skin & wound assist

Upload cut, rash, acne, burn. Gives general observation, possible similar conditions (non-definitive), self-care, red flags for doctor visit. August AI style guardrails baked in.

⚠️ This is general information only, not a diagnosis. Encourages seeing dermatologist.
⏰

Medication Scheduler

Each medicine card has "Add to Scheduler". Pick times based on frequency (1/day = 1 timestamp etc). Emails you the card with precautions & safety guardrails.

🌐

11 Languages

English, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Gujarati, Spanish, French, German + more. UI + responses adapt.

🛡️

Safety Guardrails

Emergency detection (chest pain, breathing difficulty, suicidal thoughts), no definitive diagnosis, no prescription of new drugs, always disclaimer, encourage professional care.

Flow

in 4 calm steps.

01

Create profile & accept disclaimer

First time only: see disclaimer, terms, privacy. Add age, weight, height, contacts, preferred language, health conditions, allergies.

02

Ask however you want

Type, upload prescription image/PDF, upload skin photo, or hold mic to speak in your language. We store chat in SQLite.

03

Verified answer generation

ChromaDB searched → if relevant, used. Else Gemini 1.5 Flash (your 3.6 key) answers in simple non-hallucinated language + guardrails.

04

Act safely

Medicine cards → add to scheduler → pick timestamps → email notification with card + precautions. Always see doctor for personal decisions.
