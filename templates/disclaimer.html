Important Safety Information
Care Companion is an AI health information assistant, not a medical professional. All content is for educational purpose only and does NOT replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. No definitive diagnosis, no new prescriptions. Always consult qualified healthcare provider.

🛡️ How Care Companion works

  • Knowledge-base first: Every query searches ChromaDB medical knowledge. If answer found, it is used and cited. If not, Gemini 1.5 Flash (Gemini 3.6 Flash compatible) generates simple, non-hallucinated answer.
  • Multimodal: Text, image (prescription PDF/image, skin disease/cut), voice (Faster Whisper Large-v3) — voice response in your preferred language set in profile.
  • Prescription parser: Extracts Medicine name, Dosage, Frequency, Duration, Route, Instructions + general precaution. One-click add to scheduler.
  • Skin assist: Gives general info, possible similar conditions (non-definitive), precautions, red flags. Never definitive diagnosis.
  • Medication scheduler: Email reminder via profile email with medicine card + safety guardrails.

⚠️ Safety Guardrails

  • Emergency keywords (chest pain, difficulty breathing, suicidal thoughts, stroke, heart attack) trigger immediate emergency redirect (call 112 India).
  • No dosage prescribing beyond your existing prescription.
  • Always includes disclaimer, encourages seeing doctor/pharmacist.
  • Respects profile: allergies, health conditions, preferred language.
  • Data: SQLite for auth & chat, ChromaDB for vectors. Private to user.

📜 Terms & Privacy (summary)

  • You provide accurate profile info (name, age, weight, height, email, contact, emergency contact, language, health conditions, allergies optional).
  • You will NOT upload harmful, illegal, or non-medical NSFW content.
  • You understand AI may make mistakes; verify with professionals.
  • We do not share your data. Emails only for medication reminders.
  • This is not emergency service. For emergency call 112 / local ER.
You can review safety info anytime in chat. Profile language controls response language.
